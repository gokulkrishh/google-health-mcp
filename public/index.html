Sign in with Google

Add it to any AI app

Paste your server address, click Connect, and type your access token on the page that opens. That's it.

Add a custom connector (some apps call it an MCP server) with your server address. The app opens a sign-in page on your own server, which asks for your access token (MCP_HTTP_TOKEN). Type it there and click Allow access. The token never goes into a chat, a URL or the app's settings.

In Claude it's Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, and it then works in the desktop and phone apps too. In ChatGPT, turn on Developer mode under Settings → Apps & Connectors and choose OAuth. In Grok, use grok.com/connectors. In Claude Code, run the command shown, then /mcp → Authenticate.

Apps without sign-in support can send the token as a header or with ?key= instead.

What your AI can read

Eight tools, all read-only.

get_daily_summaryOne day at a glance: steps, distance, calories, Active Zone Minutes, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep and workouts.
get_daily_rollupTrends per day or week, up to 90 days.
get_rollupHour-by-hour values, such as heart rate during a run.
get_data_pointsRaw records: sleep stages, workouts, weight, SpO2, HRV.
get_exercise_routeA workout's GPS route with distance, pace, elevation and heart rate.
get_user_infoProfile, units, time zone and goals.
list_paired_devicesYour devices, battery level and last sync.
list_data_typesEvery readable data type and what it supports.

Troubleshooting

The common fixes.

"Not signed in"

Use Sign in with Google. You only need it again if Google ends the sign-in.

"Unauthorized" or "credential rejected"

The token in your app is wrong or was changed. Update it in the app's connector settings.

No data for today

Open the Fitbit app so your tracker syncs. Sleep and resting heart rate appear after a night's wear.
